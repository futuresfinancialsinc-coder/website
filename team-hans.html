Hans Patel

Co-Founder & Co-CEO

Hans Patel is a business and economics leader studying in the Financial Technology Academy as a senior at Bergen County Technical High School in Teterboro. He is enthusiastic about the intersection between labor and financial markets in the AI driven economy.

As a co-founder and Co-Chief Executive Officer of Futures Financials, Hans directs strategy, operations, leading the research initiative, and expanding the demographic reach to non-English immigrants, Indigenous peoples, and incarcerated juveniles. His work has been recognized by the International Economics Olympiad OpenTrack, National Economics Challenge, Future Business Leaders of America, and the Money Awareness & Inclusion Awards.
