Danny Jang

Founder & Board Director

Danny Jang is an applied mathematics and economics leader pursuing his bachelor’s degree as a freshman at Harvard University. He is enthusiastic about financial markets, economics, youth leadership, and behavioral economics.

As the Founder and Board Director of Futures Financials, Danny directs strategy, growth, and program expansion across high school and collegiate chapters, while conducting economics and sustainability research at Harvard Business School, MIT, and Brandeis. His work has been recognized nationally by the Money Awareness and Inclusion Awards, FinMango, and the Bryan Cameron Education Foundation.
