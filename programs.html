Our Curricula

Futures Financials teaches three lessons, one for each stage of school, from grade 4 through grade 12.

Every session is interactive, with questions throughout, a game at the end, and the memes and trends students actually follow.

Request a Lesson
  • Grades 4–6 Financial Foundations
  • Grades 7–8 Entrepreneurship
  • Grades 9–12 Credit & Investing
01

Financial Foundations Money habits that stick.

Grades 4–6

The money vocabulary and everyday habits everything else is built on.

02

Entrepre­neurship Ideas into income.

Grades 7–8

Applying those basics to build, price and run a real idea.

03

Credit & Investing Decisions that compound.

Grades 9–12

Credit, investing and the decisions students face heading into adulthood.
