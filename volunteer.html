A student-led movement for financial literacy.

Every lesson we teach starts with a volunteer.

Ready to make a difference?

Volunteering with Futures Financials means joining 450+ students across 7 countries who teach money skills to the next generation. You’ll help younger students build habits that last, and grow as a teacher, leader, and communicator along the way.

Step 1: Choose Your Team

Pick one of our two volunteer teams and fill out its registration form.

Education Team

Lead workshops, build presentations, mentor students and write the material: budgeting, investing, credit, entrepreneurship, economics.

Registration Form

Outreach & Partnerships Team

Organize events, build partnerships with schools and organizations, run social campaigns and get our work in front of more students.

Registration Form

Step 2: Watch Your Inbox

Once you’re accepted, your team lead will email you your first assignment and everything you need to get started.

Step 3: Earn Service Hours

Eligible volunteer hours count toward community service and leadership recognition.
