Privacy Policy

We built this site to collect nothing. This page explains exactly what that means and the few places where data does change hands.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

The short version

Futuresfinancials.org does not collect, store, or sell your personal information. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no accounts on this website. You can read every page here without giving us anything.

Information we do not collect

We do not use cookies or similar storage technologies. We do not run Google Analytics or any other analytics service. We do not use advertising or social media tracking pixels. We do not have user accounts, logins, or newsletters hosted on this site.

Our forms

Several pages on this site have forms, including volunteer applications, chapter applications, partnership inquiries, and story submissions. These forms do not send anything to a server we control.

When you press submit, the form opens your own email program with a draft message already filled in using what you typed. Nothing is sent until you choose to send that email yourself. At that point your message arrives in our inbox the same way any other email would, and it is handled by our email provider under their privacy terms.

If you never send the draft, we never receive your information.

What our email inbox receives

If you do email us, whether through a form draft or directly, we receive whatever you chose to include. That typically means your name, your email address, and the details you wrote about volunteering, starting a chapter, partnering with us, or sharing a story.

We use that information only to reply to you and to run the program you asked about. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone outside Futures Financials Inc. except where we are required to by law.

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by Vercel. Like nearly all web hosts, Vercel automatically records basic technical request data such as IP address, browser type, and which page was requested. This happens at the hosting layer, is standard for every site on the internet, and is used for delivering the site and protecting it from abuse. We do not combine that data with anything else or use it to identify individual visitors.

Embedded and linked content

Our Programs page embeds presentation slides hosted by Canva. When that page loads, your browser contacts Canva to display the slides, and Canva may collect technical data under its own privacy policy.

Other pages link out to third party sites such as news outlets, partner organizations, Instagram, and LinkedIn. Those are ordinary links. Clicking one takes you to a site we do not control, governed by that site's own privacy policy.

Students and children

Futures Financials teaches students, and some of them are under 13. This website is an informational site about our organization and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Our programs are arranged through schools, libraries, and partner organizations rather than through this website. If you believe a child has sent us personal information and you would like it removed, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

Because we do not collect anything automatically, there is nothing to opt out of. If you have emailed us and you want your message and details deleted from our inbox, write to futuresfinancialsinc@gmail.com and ask. We will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add analytics, a hosted form service, or online donations, we will update this page before or when that change goes live, and we will update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy can go to futuresfinancialsinc@gmail.com. Futures Financials Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, EIN 33-1515809.
