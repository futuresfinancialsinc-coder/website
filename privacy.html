Privacy Policy

How Futures Financials Inc. handles information on this website, and why there is very little of it to handle.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Overview

Futures Financials Inc. is committed to protecting the privacy of the students, volunteers, educators, and partner organizations who visit this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

This website is unusual in one important respect. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or user accounts, and it collects no personal information automatically. You can read every page on this site without providing us with anything.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily choose to send it to us. This happens when you email us directly or when you use one of the inquiry forms on our Volunteer, Start a Chapter, Our Partners, or Success Stories pages.

The information we receive is limited to what you choose to include, which typically consists of your name, your email address, your school or organization, and the details of your message. We do not request or require sensitive information such as financial account numbers, government identification numbers, or health information.

How Our Forms Work

The forms on this website do not transmit data to a server that we operate. When you select submit, the form opens your own email application with a draft message composed from the information you entered. Nothing is transmitted to us until you choose to send that message yourself.

If you close the draft without sending it, we never receive your information. Once you send the message, it arrives in our email inbox in the same manner as any other email and is handled by our email provider in accordance with its own privacy practices.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you send us solely to respond to your inquiry and to administer the program you asked about, such as reviewing a volunteer application, evaluating a proposed chapter, or coordinating a partnership.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease personal information to third parties. We do not share it with anyone outside Futures Financials Inc., except where disclosure is required by law or is necessary to protect the safety of an individual.

Cookies and Automatic Information

This website does not set cookies and does not use local storage, session storage, analytics services, advertising pixels, or any other tracking technology.

Our website is hosted by Vercel Inc. As is standard for all internet hosting, Vercel automatically records basic technical request information, which may include IP address, browser type, and the page requested. This occurs at the hosting layer, is used to deliver the website and protect it from abuse, and is governed by Vercel's own privacy policy. We do not access this data to identify individual visitors and we do not combine it with any other information.

Third-Party Websites and Links

Our Programs page embeds presentation materials hosted by Canva. When that page loads, your browser requests content directly from Canva, which may collect technical information in accordance with its own privacy policy.

This website also links to external websites, including news organizations, partner organizations, and our social media profiles. These are ordinary hyperlinks. We do not control those websites and we are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any website you visit.

Children's Privacy

Futures Financials provides financial education to students, and some of the students we serve are under the age of 13. This website is an informational resource about our organization and it is not directed at children, nor does it knowingly collect personal information from children.

Participation in our programs is arranged through schools, libraries, and partner organizations rather than through this website. If you believe that a child has sent personal information to us and you would like that information removed, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Retention and Your Choices

Because this website does not collect information automatically, there is nothing to opt out of when you browse it. Messages you send to us are retained in our email inbox only as long as they remain relevant to the program or conversation they concern.

You may request that we delete your message and the information it contains at any time by writing to us at the address below, and we will honor that request.

International Users

Futures Financials Inc. operates from the United States, and our chapters extend to several other countries. If you contact us from outside the United States, please understand that your message will be received and stored in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our organization grows or as this website adds new functionality. If we introduce analytics, a hosted form service, or online donations, we will revise this policy before or at the time that change takes effect, and we will update the date shown at the top of this page.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how we handle your information, please contact us at futuresfinancialsinc@gmail.com. Futures Financials Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, EIN 33-1515809.
