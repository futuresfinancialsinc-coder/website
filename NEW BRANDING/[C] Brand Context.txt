2. VOICE AND VIBE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Youth-led, but never amateur. Run by high school students, reaching 15,000+ of
them. The brand has to earn trust with principals, funders and reporters while
still looking like something a teenager would actually follow.

THE ONE-LINE TEST: if a superintendent and a sixteen-year-old would both take it
seriously, it is on brand. If only one of them would, it is not.

WE ARE
  Credible first.      Finance is the subject. Nothing can look improvised.
  Warm, not corporate. Growth, not banks. The mark is a plant, not a vault.
  Plain-spoken.        Short sentences, real numbers, no jargon.
  Evidence-led.        15,000 students. 70+ schools. 7 countries. Say the number.

WE ARE NOT
  A startup.      No neon gradients, no 3D blobs, no "disrupting."
  A bank.         No navy-and-grey, no stock handshakes, no skylines.
  A school club.  No clip art, no emoji in body copy, no rainbow palettes.
  Vague.          "Empowering the next generation" says nothing. Cut it.

WRITING RULES
  - No em dashes in site prose. Use commas, colons or a full stop.
  - Page titles use a pipe:  Futures Financials | About Us
  - Never invent bios, quotes, program copy or statistics. Placeholders stay
    placeholders until a human fills them.
  - AI-generated imagery must be clearly stylized and labelled "Illustration."
    Media-coverage cards only ever get real photos.

3. COLOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Two families only: a green spine and a gold accent, over near-black and
off-white neutrals. Every hex was sampled from the logo artwork by sorting its
pixels by hue and taking the median of each group. These are the colors the
mark actually contains.

  #193e20   green-900   Hero overlays, dark sections.
  #22592d   green-800   Deep section backgrounds.

  #218f47   green-600   Sampled from the logo leaves.

  #b8890f   gold-900    The only gold safe as text on white.
  #faf3df   gold-50     Warm tint for callouts.

  #0b1612   outline     Logo stroke color. Illustration only.
  #56675d   muted       Captions, secondary text.
  #e3ebe6   line        Borders and dividers.

KNOWN GAP
The logo's gold is #f8a31c. The site has been using #d9a728, which is duller and
browner. The logo's greens are also brighter than #2a843c. Keep both ranges:
bright values for artwork and gradients, deep values for interface and text.
But stop treating them as the same color.

THE GRADIENT
One recipe, used on every branded asset. Always horizontal. No vertical
component, no diagonal.

  background: linear-gradient(90deg, #5ed555 0%, #3eb452 34%, #30a34c 72%, #229145 100%);

CONTRAST - MEASURED, NOT GUESSED

  Foreground   Background         Ratio   Verdict
  ----------   ----------------   -----   ----------------------------------
  White        #193e20            11.99   AAA, anything
  White        #22592d             8.28   AAA, anything
  White        #1e7b42             5.29   AA, body text OK
  White        #218f47             4.13   Large text only (18pt+)
  White        #37ae4f             2.87   FAIL - never put text here
  White        #48c251             2.30   FAIL - never put text here

  #f8a31c      #193e20             5.85   AA, body text OK

THE RULE THAT FALLS OUT OF THIS: bright greens are for backgrounds and artwork.
The moment someone needs to read a sentence, you are on #1e7b42 or darker.

4. TYPOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DISPLAY: PLAYFAIR DISPLAY
  Chosen 1 November 2026. Google Fonts, free, 400-900 plus italics.
  High-contrast transitional serif.
  Why: verified by reading the live font-family on the peer sites. Origami For
  Good and FLY to the World both run Playfair Display, and it is the closest
  free web equivalent to the Bodoni used in the first round of LinkedIn artwork.

BODY: OPEN SAUCE ONE
  Already self-hosted at 400 / 500 / 700 / 800 in /fonts. Clean geometric
  humanist sans that stays out of a serif headline's way. Also what Cancer Kids
  First runs.

RETIRE BALOO 2
  It conflicts with a serif display face. Drop it, or demote it to big impact
  numerals only (the "15,000+" treatment) and nothing else.

TYPE RULES
  Headlines         Playfair Display, sentence case. Never all-caps for a full
                    sentence.
  Wordmark lockups  All-caps, letterspaced 0.12-0.18em, hairline rule beneath.
  Small labels      All-caps, letterspaced 0.2em, 11-13px. For "NONPROFIT",
                    section eyebrows, stat captions.
  Body              Open Sauce One 400, 16-18px, line-height 1.6, max 70
                    characters per line.

LICENSING TRAP
  The first LinkedIn banner and experience card were rendered in Bodoni 72 and
  Big Caslon, which ship with macOS. Fine for images exported locally. They
  CANNOT be embedded on the website or opened by a designer on Windows.
  Anything new goes in Playfair Display.

5. LOGO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A dollar coin sprouting from a stack of gold bars. Growth, not storage.
Keep it full color.

DO
  - Clear space: at least the height of the top coin, empty, on all four sides.
  - Minimum size: 48px tall on screen, 0.6in in print. Below that the sparkles
    fill in and it turns to mush.
  - Backgrounds: white, #f2f8f3, or any green from the palette. It reads on all.
  - Use the transparent PNG for cut-outs.

NEVER
  - Flatten it to one color. The detail is defined by color, not outline, so a
    white or gold silhouette collapses into a blob. This was tested. It does
    not work.
  - Recolor the coins. Gold is doing the work.
  - Add a drop shadow, outer glow or stroke.
  - Stretch, rotate or crop it.
  - Place the square green version on a different green. The two fields will
    not match.
  - Set it on a photo without a disc or panel behind it.

6. PATTERN AND MOTIF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Background texture on branded assets is built from the logo's own vocabulary:
coins marked $, two-leaf sprouts, coin stacks, sparkle bursts. Outlines only.

  Color     White outline, 14-17% opacity, never higher. Benchmarked against
            Origami For Good's crane pattern, whose outlines sit only 20-40
            levels above their background. The restraint is the whole effect.
  Stroke    About 1.9px at 1x resolution.
  Scale     9-16% of canvas height, rotated freely, allowed to run off edges.
  Density   Scattered, never tiled on a visible grid.

                                            top and bottom.

  Instagram square            1080 x 1080   Badge layout works unchanged.
  Favicon                     32/192/512    Coin only. The full mark is
                                            unreadable this small.

BANNER PHOTO TREATMENT
  Duotone every photo onto a single green luminance ramp (#091e0d to #b0dab8)
  so multiple classrooms read as one field rather than a contact sheet.

BADGE RULE
  The disc must stay the brightest element on the card. If it matches the
  field, only the ring shows.
