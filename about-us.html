About Us

Scroll

We teach the money skills school leaves out.

We challenge conventional thinking about who can lead, who can learn, and how young people build money skills that last.

Community at its core

How we teach

Our volunteers learn as much as they teach. They grow as educators, as mentors, and as people who understand money.

18 chapters. 7 countries.

Run by students, for students, in classrooms and libraries on four continents.

What we stand for

  • Why we exist

    Founded in 2022 to reimagine how young people learn about money, and to prove students can build financially confident futures themselves.

  • What we do

    Interactive workshops, resources, mentorship and real-world experience, delivered in a way that actually lands with young people.

  • How we do it

    450+ student volunteers across 7 countries, through 18 chapters.
