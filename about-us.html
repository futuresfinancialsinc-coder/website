Who are we?

We are a youth-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit that transforms financial education into accessible, pop-culture driven content for young people from diverse backgrounds.

EIN #33-1515809

Our Why

Founded in 2022, Futures Financials (FF) was created to reimagine how young people learn about money, and to prove that students can build financially confident futures themselves.

Our What

We teach the money skills school leaves out through interactive workshops, resources, mentorship, and real-world experience, delivered in a way that actually lands with young people.

Our How

Through 18+ chapters across 7 countries, more than 450 student volunteers have taught over 15,000 students in 70+ schools and libraries.

The Ripple Effect

A money lesson rarely stays in the room where it was taught. It travels home, to friends, and into a first paycheck.

When a student brings a budget home, the whole household starts planning differently. When teenagers lead the class, adults rethink who can teach. When a new chapter opens abroad, the lessons are rebuilt for a new culture and keep spreading.

At Futures Financials

We believe students learn money best from other students.

We challenge conventional thinking about who can lead, who can learn, and how young people build money skills that last.

“I thought investing was basically gambling. Now I research long-term investing instead of chasing quick profits.”

Community at the Core

Our chapters bring together students from different schools, neighborhoods, and countries, so every lesson is shaped by the people in the room.

Our volunteers learn as much as they teach. They grow as educators, as mentors, and as people who understand money.
