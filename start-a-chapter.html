What are FF chapters?

Local leaders teaching
money skills that last

A Futures Financials chapter is a local branch of our nonprofit: a group of students who teach financial literacy to younger students in their own community.

Chapters range from 6 to 50+ members and can be formed as school clubs, community groups, or regional initiatives.

Chapter Application

Getting started

How to create a Futures Financials chapter

  1. 1

    Prepare Your Foundation

    If your chapter is based at a school, talk with your administration and get preliminary approval before you apply.

  2. 2

    Submit Your Application

    Complete the chapter application and hear back within one week. Not every submission can be accepted.

  3. 3

    Interview with Our Team

    If you advance, we’ll set up an interview to talk through your goals and your vision for impact in your community.

Questions about the application process?
