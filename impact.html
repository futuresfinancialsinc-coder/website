Our Reach Beyond Borders

While the list above shows where our chapters teach, every session reaches further than the room it happens in: a community of students sharing what they learned with friends and family online.

On Instagram, we share moments from the classroom, behind-the-scenes looks at chapter events and press, and easy ways for students to volunteer or start a chapter of their own.

Follow our journey

Every session we teach has two effects: what a student does with money this week, and the habits they carry for years.

Building Money Confidence

Interactive lessons on budgeting, saving, credit, and investing turn abstract ideas into decisions students make for themselves.

Pop-culture examples and real-life scenarios keep students engaged, so the lesson sticks after the bell rings.

Inspiring the Next Generation

Student volunteers learn to teach, mentor, and lead, building skills most people do not practice until adulthood.

Explaining a concept to a full classroom deepens their own understanding of money and how to use it.

Reaching Schools & Libraries

We bring ready-to-run financial literacy sessions to classrooms and public libraries where students might never get one otherwise.

Teachers and librarians gain a student-led partner that fits lessons to the age and needs of the room.

Growing Chapters Worldwide

Chapters let students start Futures Financials in their own school or community and adapt lessons to the people they teach.

Each new chapter extends our reach while keeping the model that makes it work: students teaching students.
